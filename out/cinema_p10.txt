Design Patterns: Adapter
Adapter Pattern
Context/problem & solution: 
Sending account emails only has Spring's JavaMailSender available, which works in terms of generic SimpleMailMessage objects (not 
something AuthController or PasswordService should need to know how to construct, or which from-address and URL to wire in every time).
EmailService and AccountEmailService sit between the callers and JavaMailSender, adapting its generic send(SimpleMailMessage) API into 
small, purpose-built methods: sendConfirmationEmail(toEmail, firstName, token), sendPasswordResetEmail(toEmail, firstName, token), 
sendPasswordChangedEmail(toEmail, firstName). Callers depend only on these domain-specific methods; if the mail transport ever changed 
(for ex, swapping JavaMailSender for a third-party email API), only the two adapter classes would need to change, not every caller.
SOLID principles achieved:
Single Responsibility: each adapter owns the wording and structure of its own specific email; JavaMailSender only knows generic mail 
transport.
Dependency Inversion: callers depend on a small abstraction close to their own needs (EmailService/AccountEmailService), not directly on 
JavaMailSender/SimpleMailMessage.
Open/Closed: new email types can be added as new adapter methods without modifying JavaMailSender or any calling controller/service.
